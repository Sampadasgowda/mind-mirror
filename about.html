About us

We believe everyone deserves to understand themselves better without hitting a paywall. Our library of personality, emotion and relationship tests is free forever, with complete results for every test.

How the tests work

Instead of asking you to rate abstract statements, each test puts you in everyday situations and asks what you would actually do. Every response is weighted on a specific dimension, such as Openness or Self-Regulation, and your choices are combined into a profile with plain-language explanations.

All scenarios are original and inspired by established psychological frameworks such as the Big Five model, emotional intelligence research and attachment theory.

Why create an account?

  • Unlock full results for every test
  • Keep all your results in one dashboard
  • Retake tests and see how you change over time

An important note

These tests are for self-reflection and fun. They are not medical, psychological or diagnostic tools. If you're struggling with your mental health, please reach out to a qualified professional.
