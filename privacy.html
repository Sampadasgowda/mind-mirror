Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and an encrypted password when you sign up.
  • Test results: your answers to each test you complete while signed in, and the date you took it.
  • Browser storage: unfinished test progress is kept in your own browser so you can resume. It is not sent to us.

How we use it

We use your information only to run your account: signing you in, showing your results, and sending account emails such as confirmations and password resets. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Where it's stored

Accounts and results are stored with our infrastructure provider, Supabase. Access is restricted so that only you can read your own results.

Sharing

Your results are private unless you choose to copy a share link. Anyone who opens a share link must be signed in to view it.
